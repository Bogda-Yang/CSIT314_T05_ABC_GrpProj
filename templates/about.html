Project Overview

A person-to-person fundraising platform for urgent, transparent support.

FireflyFund is a person-to-person fundraising platform designed to help individuals and families seek support for urgent needs such as medical treatment, education, emergencies, and daily hardship. The platform allows fundraisers to create campaigns, upload images, set goals and deadlines, and submit campaigns for administrator approval. Donors can browse campaigns, view transparent donation records, save favourites, recharge wallet balance, and support causes directly. Admin users manage campaign approval, user accounts, categories, and platform reports.

Vision & Mission

Small acts of kindness, made visible, trusted, and meaningful.

Vision

Our vision is to make small acts of kindness visible, trusted, and meaningful. Like fireflies in the dark, every donation can become a small spark that helps light someone's path forward.

Mission

Our mission is to build a transparent and accessible fundraising system where people can confidently give to real human needs. FireflyFund aims to connect fundraisers and supporters through clear campaign information, public donation records, approval control, and user-friendly tools for giving, managing, and tracking impact.

Project Values

The principles that shape how FireflyFund is designed, used, and managed.

Transparency

Donation records, campaign progress, fundraiser information, and approval status are made visible so users can understand where support goes.

Trust

Campaigns are reviewed before publication, and user actions are structured to reduce misuse and improve confidence.

Accessibility

The platform is designed to be simple for fundraisers, donors, and administrators to use across different pages and workflows.

Community Support

FireflyFund focuses on direct human help, encouraging many small contributions to create meaningful impact.

Responsibility

The system supports account management, campaign moderation, category control, and reporting so the platform can be managed safely and consistently.
